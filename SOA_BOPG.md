## 16. Conclusion and Future Development: The Overall Conclusion of Structure Over Axioms

$$

\text{Structure}\rightarrow\text{Representation}\rightarrow\text{Operation}\rightarrow\text{Axiom}

$$

rather than allowing predefined axioms alone to determine all expressible structures.
